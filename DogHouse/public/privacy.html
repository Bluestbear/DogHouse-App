🐕 DogHouse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

DogHouse is a playful app for couples. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address (for sign-in), display name, and chosen avatar.
  • App activity: doghouse events (who sent whom, the reason and make-it-up note you type), belly rubs, votes, reactions, roasts, friendships, and in-app purchases made with virtual treats.
  • Push token: a device identifier used solely to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Usage data: which screens you open and which in-app actions you take (for example that a sending happened, not what you wrote), plus a record of when the app was opened. This is used only to understand how DogHouse is used and to improve it. See Usage data below — you can turn it off.
  • Crash reports: diagnostics sent when the app crashes, so it can be fixed.

What we don't collect

  • No location data, no contacts, and no advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we show no ads.
  • The text you write — doghouse reasons, make-it-up notes, roasts — is never included in usage data. It stays between you and your partner.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Who can see your activity

  • Doghouse events are private between you and your partner unless the sender explicitly marks a sending as shared with friends.
  • Shared sendings are visible only to the sender's friends within the app.
  • Your profile name and avatar are visible to people you add as friends.

Usage data

DogHouse measures how the app is used with Google Analytics for Firebase, configured without advertising identifiers. We record events such as opening a tab, sending someone to the doghouse, letting them out, or buying a cosmetic, along with a record of when the app was opened. We do not record the words you type, and we never sell this data or use it for advertising.

You can switch it off at any time: Profile → Share Usage Data. Turning it off stops both the analytics events and the record of app opens. Notifications and every other part of the app keep working.

Reports and blocks

If you report a sending, a photo or a person, the report (what you reported, the reason you picked and anything you wrote) is emailed to the developer and kept until it has been handled. The person you reported is not told who reported them. Your block list is stored on your account, visible only to you, and is used to hide blocked people's content from you.

Where your data lives

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication) in Google's European data centers, protected by security rules so only you, your partner, and the friends you choose can access what's yours.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently erases your profile, history, friendships, feed posts, and sign-in account, and unpairs your partner. Deletion is processed immediately and cannot be undone.
